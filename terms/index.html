By downloading or using Binny you agree to these terms.

01The app

Binny watches the size of your Mac's Trash and, past a limit you choose, draws a simulated blanket over the Trash icon in your Dock. It is provided free of charge.

02Your files

Binny never deletes anything on its own. It empties the Trash only when you click Empty Trash and confirm. Emptying the Trash permanently deletes the files in it, as it does from Finder, and we cannot recover them.
